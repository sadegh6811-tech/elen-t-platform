# Journal Selection Guide

## Primary Candidates

### 1. BioSystems (Elsevier)
- Impact Factor: ~2.0
- Scope: Theoretical biology, information in biological systems
- Fit: Excellent
- Review time: 4-8 weeks
- URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/biosystems

### 2. Entropy (MDPI)
- Impact Factor: ~2.7
- Scope: Information theory, thermodynamics
- Fit: Very good
- Review time: 3-6 weeks
- APC: ~2000 CHF
- URL: https://www.mdpi.com/journal/entropy

### 3. Foundations of Physics (Springer)
- Impact Factor: ~1.5
- Scope: Foundations of quantum mechanics
- Fit: Good
- Review time: 8-12 weeks
- URL: https://www.springer.com/journal/10701

## Secondary Candidates

### 4. Journal of Consciousness Studies
- Scope: Consciousness, mind-matter interface
- Review time: 8-16 weeks

### 5. Quantum Reports (MDPI)
- Scope: Quantum biology, quantum information
- Review time: 3-5 weeks

### 6. Water (MDPI)
- Scope: Water science
- Note: May not accept consciousness claims

## Recommended Strategy

1. First choice: BioSystems
2. If rejected: Entropy
3. If rejected: Foundations of Physics

## Timeline

- Week 1-2: Finalize manuscript
- Week 3: Submit
- Week 4-12: Review
- Week 13-15: Revision
- Total: 3-5 months to first decision

## Preprint Option

Consider OSF Preprints before submission for priority.
